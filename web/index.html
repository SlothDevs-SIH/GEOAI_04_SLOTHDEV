Why radar · honest limits

Monsoon floods happen under cloud, where optical satellites are blind. Calm flood water reflects radar away from the satellite, so it shows up dark.

Output is an open-water flood-extent estimate: flooded crops and streets can look bright on radar and may be missed. Single-date result shown; the full M8 compares before/after scenes.
